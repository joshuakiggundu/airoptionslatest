FAQ

Pricing Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in an AC maintenance visit?

Every visit includes a full inspection of your HVAC equipment, a service report, and access to discounted spares and accessories, with more frequent plans offering priority scheduling and free minor repairs.

How do I choose the right plan?

If you have a single unit needing an annual check, the Essential Plan covers a yearly tune-up. For homes or offices with higher usage or multiple units, our higher-frequency plans (Advantage, Premium, and Elite) offer more inspections, longer warranties, and greater savings on spares.

Are these prices per unit or per visit?

Pricing is per visit, per unit or system — so a home or office with multiple AC units will be quoted based on the total number of units serviced.

Do you offer custom pricing for commercial or industrial clients?

Yes. For large commercial or industrial installations, contact us directly for a tailored quote based on your equipment and service needs.
