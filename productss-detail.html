Our Multi-Split Air Conditioning System lets you cool or heat multiple rooms independently — all from a single outdoor unit. Each indoor unit runs on its own settings, so you get precise, room-by-room comfort without the cost or clutter of multiple outdoor condensers. Inverter technology adjusts compressor speed to match real-time demand, cutting energy consumption compared to conventional systems. Available in wall-mounted, ceiling cassette, and ducted configurations, it's a flexible fit for homes, offices, and commercial spaces throughout Kampala.

Key Features

  1. Multiple Indoor Units:One outdoor unit powers up to 8 indoor units, giving you custom, room-by-room comfort without extra outdoor condensers.
  2. Individual Temperature Control:Set a different temperature (or turn units off entirely) in each room, so you're never cooling empty spaces.
  3. Space Saving:A single compact outdoor unit keeps your exterior clutter-free, even as coverage expands to more rooms.
  4. Energy Efficient:Inverter-driven compressor adjusts output to match real demand, reducing energy consumption versus running multiple standalone units.
  5. Flexible Installation:Mix indoor unit types (wall-mounted, ceiling cassette, ducted) to fit different rooms and layouts in the same system.
