FAQ

AC Maintenance Questions

How often should I service my AC unit?

We recommend servicing your AC at least once a year, ideally before peak usage season. Homes and offices with heavier use benefit from more frequent visits, which is why we offer maintenance plans ranging from one to six visits per year.

What does an AC maintenance visit include?

A standard visit includes a full inspection of your unit, cleaning of filters and coils, checking refrigerant levels, and a service report. Higher-tier plans add benefits like priority scheduling, extended warranties, and free minor repairs.

Why is regular maintenance important?

Regular maintenance improves energy efficiency, prevents unexpected breakdowns, extends the lifespan of your system, and helps catch small issues before they become costly repairs.

Do you offer maintenance plans for businesses?

Yes. We work with homes, offices, and institutions across Kampala, and can tailor a maintenance schedule to fit your building's size and usage.
